The key insight: on the worst days, most damage happens before the market even opens. March 9, 2020 gapped down −7.25% at the open — then actually closed up 0.45% from that open. Anyone who could only trade market hours made money that day, yet the index "fell 6.8%". Meanwhile March 16 was the true horror: a −6.2% gap followed by another −6.4% intraday bleed — no bounce, pure capitulation. Also notice the reversal pattern: several of the biggest gap-downs (Apr 7 2025, Feb 28 2020, Aug 5 2024) finished flat or positive — violent overnight panic often marks short-term exhaustion. Across all 66 days that fell more than 3%, the overnight gap averaged −1.65% and the intraday session −2.51%.
